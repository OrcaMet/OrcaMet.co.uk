Why It Works

This service was built on the needs of real racing teams, where a missed shift or wrong side of the course isn't academic, it's the difference between winning and trailing the fleet. We've sat in the nav seat, worked under pressure, and know what matters when every degree counts.

Forecasts are always aligned with your sailing area, boat type, risk tolerance, and onboard systems. Whether it's a simple gut check the night before, or full start-to-finish support, you get what you need: no more, no less.

We have sailed offshore in serious conditions and value the input of a calm and informed source of information. This is key to keeping sailing safe, fun and prepared.
