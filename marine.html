Forecasting Built for Marine Decision-Making

From offshore wind construction to regular oil rig operations, OrcaMet's tools adapt to your mission profile and vessel risk profile. We understand that marine operations require more than just weather data, they need actionable solutions that consider vessel limitations, operational thresholds, and safety margins.

Our forecasts are tailored to your specific operational requirements, whether that's jack-up vessel operations in marginal conditions, critical heavy lift windows, or optimizing transit routes for fuel efficiency. Every forecast considers your vessel's motion limits, crane capacities, and operational constraints.
